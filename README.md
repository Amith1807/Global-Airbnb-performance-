An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyse Airbnb’s global marketplace performance across listings, hosts, reviews, pricing, ratings, and customer behaviour. The project transforms Airbnb listing and review data into an intuitive business intelligence solution designed to identify market trends, performance drivers, and opportunities for improvement.

The dashboard is structured into multiple analytical perspectives. The Growth Analysis explores Airbnb’s listing expansion over time, highlighting major growth periods, market maturity, regulatory impacts, and the disruption caused by COVID-19. It also analyses the distribution of property types and the evolution of different accommodation categories.

The Market & City Analysis compares major Airbnb markets based on listing volumes, reviews, average prices, host performance, and overall ratings. The analysis identifies high-performing cities and demonstrates how a relatively small number of major markets contribute a significant share of global activity. Pricing differences across room types provide additional insight into the structure of the Airbnb marketplace.

The Ratings & Customer Behaviour section evaluates overall and detailed customer ratings, including cleanliness, accuracy, communication, location, and value for money. Review-frequency analysis provides insight into customer engagement and identifies unusual review behaviour that may require further investigation.

A dedicated Trust & Host Analysis evaluates host identity verification and profile-picture presence using calculated DAX measures. This helps assess trust signals within the Airbnb ecosystem and highlights the relationship between host verification and profile completeness.
